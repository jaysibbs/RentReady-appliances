Who it helps

A practical route for property teams and everyday buyers.

Most sellers list stock. RentalReady starts with the property requirement: what the kitchen needs, how quickly it must be ready, and what finish the client expects.

For construction firms Support handover with appliance choices that match the finish.

Batch and package requests help new-build, refurbishment, and fit-out teams source appliances around deadlines, access, and agreed specification.

For letting agencies Send one brief instead of chasing individual listings.

Share the property list, postcode, access notes, quality tier, and target move-in date. We turn that into a practical quote request.

For property managers Keep standards consistent across repeat replacements.

Portfolio requests can be grouped by appliance type, size, colour, brand level, condition tier, and urgency.

For first-time landlords Understand the trade-offs before spending.

Choose a sensible starting point, then upgrade only where the property, tenant expectation, or viewing standard justifies it.

Quality tiers

Order by the finish the property needs.

Starter prices are low because we source by property need, not showroom stock. The final recommendation depends on brand, size, age, condition, colour, energy preference, and availability.

Value

Lowest workable starting point

Clean and tested, may show visible wear, best for budget turnovers, garages, utility spaces, and urgent replacements.

Standard

Balanced rental-ready condition

Cleaner finish, dependable brands where possible, suitable for most rental homes and move-in ready kitchens.

Premium

Better brand or stronger visual finish

Premium finish requests where the property needs a sharper look, stronger viewing impact, or a higher-end specification.

Refurbished explained

Refurbished does not mean random stock.

Refurbished options are requested by condition tier and suitability for the property. We look for clean, working appliances that make sense for the space, then confirm the trade-offs before order.

  • Brand and condition explained so buyers can choose accordingly.
  • New, graded, and refurbished options can be compared where available.
