Delivery and haul-away

Logistics are quoted separately so the scope stays clear.

Delivery and old appliance removal depend on postcode, access, timing, route availability, appliance size, and whether a batch or single item is being requested.

  • Delivery is priced separately unless a written quote clearly includes it.
  • Old appliance haul-away is a separate operation and depends on access, item size, route planning, and disposal requirements.
  • Customers should disclose stairs, tight turns, parking restrictions, lift access, narrow doorways, and fixed move-in or handover times.
  • Installation is not assumed. Electrical, plumbing, integrated fitting, and gas work may require qualified trades and should be confirmed before order.
  • Gas appliances must be tested, installed, and certified by a qualified Gas Safe engineer before use.
