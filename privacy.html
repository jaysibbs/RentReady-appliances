Privacy

Quote details are used to prepare your appliance request.

RentalReady Appliances collects the details you send through the quote form so we can understand the property, appliance need, timing, access, and preferred quality route.

  • We may collect your name, company, email, phone number, postcode, appliance request, notes, and smart quote brief. If you email photos separately, those photos are used to prepare the quote.
  • Your details are used to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote, clarify access or measurements, and keep a record of the request.
  • Quote requests are currently sent by email through FormSubmit. Do not send sensitive financial information through the form.
  • If photos are sent by email, they should only show the appliance space, access route, or item condition. Avoid including people, private documents, or unrelated personal information.
  • You can ask for your enquiry details to be deleted by contacting RentalReady Appliances using the same email address used for your quote request.
