Internal sourcing assistant

Buy against live demand, not guesswork.

This dashboard turns email, WhatsApp, and Find a Tender opportunities into buyer requests, matches those requests to John Pye-style auction lots, protects a 45% ROI target, and learns from the items you approve or reject.

Demand-first workflow The assistant only recommends stock against a saved buyer request. It does not auto-buy; every lot still needs manual condition, fee, and collection checks.
