Match enquiries to supplier products before you buy.

This dashboard turns email or WhatsApp enquiries into a sourcing brief, scores products you find on John Pye-style auction and clearance sites, and learns from the items you approve or reject.

Review-first workflow The assistant does not auto-buy or scrape suppliers. Paste candidate links, review the score, then approve the best product manually.

Sourcing shortlist
