Representative product visual notes.

The product visuals are representative examples of the appliance types RentalReady can source. Actual stock, brands, age, finish, and condition will vary by quote.

  • Washing machine and microwave inspection visuals were created from user-supplied product references, then cropped and redacted to remove auction controls, prices, website marks, lot numbers, and obvious brand identifiers.
  • Landlord turnover set visuals were prepared from user-supplied style references and cropped to remove phone, search, and source-interface elements from the visible website assets.
  • Fridge freezer, cooker, dryer, dishwasher, microwave, laundry, and appliance package visuals were prepared as a consistent RentalReady category image set so product pages stay visually uniform.
  • Visuals do not show guaranteed stock, specific brands, or exact appliance models. They show the type and quality direction customers can request.
  • Visible website pages do not show the original auction pages, bidding controls, live prices, or source website branding.
