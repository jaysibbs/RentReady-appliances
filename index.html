Item Starter Notes
Quality and condition routes

Choose Value for the lowest workable route, Standard for most rental-ready properties, or Premium for better brand strength and sharper visual finish. New, graded, and refurbished routes can be compared where available.

Delivery, haul-away, and access

Delivery and old appliance haul-away are separate operations. They are quoted by postcode, access, timing, route availability, stairs, parking, and whether specialist installation is needed.
