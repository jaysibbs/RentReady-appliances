## Day 1 Operating Rules

- Quote only items you can source, test, clean, and deliver reliably.
- Collect postcode before confirming availability.
- Track delivery and haul-away demand separately before launching those operations.
- Ask about stairs, tight doorways, electrical connection, plumbing, ventilation, and old-appliance removal interest.
- Log every lead in the CRM workbook the same day.
- Do not promise exact appliance brands unless inventory is physically confirmed.
